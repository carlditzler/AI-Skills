# Apple HIG Checklist

- HIG: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines
- Layout: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/layout
- Color: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/color
- Components: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/components
- App icons: https://developer.apple.com/design/human-interface-guidelines/app-icons

Before finalizing, check:
- Is navigation native and legible?
- Is the information hierarchy obvious at a glance?
- Does the screen still work with larger text and longer strings?
- Are system controls preferred over custom ones where possible?
